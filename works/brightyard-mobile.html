TRY IT

A phone you can actually use

Everything on the phone responds. Switch platform or theme at any time and the interface changes with it.

  1. Slide to open the doorOn Home, drag the thumb across. Let go early and it springs back. Keyboard: focus the thumb and press Enter.
  2. Book a room in a few tapsOpen Book, pick a day and a time, then tap Book on a free room. Confirm in the sheet (iOS) or the dialog (Android).
  3. Undo itThe confirmation stays visible for a few seconds as a banner (iOS) or a snackbar (Android) with an Undo action.
  4. Ask the assistantOn Assist, tap an example. It shows a proposal first. Nothing is booked until you confirm.

MICRO-INTERACTIONS

Small motion, clear meaning

Slide to open

Opening a door is a physical act, so it should take a deliberate gesture. The thumb must pass 85% of the track. Release early and it springs back in 320 ms. On success the track turns green, pulses once and says what happened and when.

Confirm layers

The sheet rises in 440 ms with a decelerating curve, so it arrives fast and settles gently. The Android dialog scales in from 92%. Both dim the screen behind, so the decision has the full focus.

New item flash

When a booking lands on Home, the new row flashes in the brand tint for two seconds. The tenant sees where the result went without a notification.

Reduced motion

With the system setting on, transitions drop to zero and the pulse, flash and shimmer stop. The meaning stays in the text and in the icons.
