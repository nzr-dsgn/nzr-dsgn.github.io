Parsing

People type money in odd ways

"8 450,50", "12.500,50" and "8,4к" become amounts. Anything else gets "Не зрозумів суму" with an example.

Safety net

A likely typo gets a question

If the total is far from the store's usual day, the bot asks "Все правильно?" before sending. Edits afterwards keep "було → стало" for the owner.

Business day

Late shifts count for yesterday

A shift closed before 05:00 belongs to the previous day. The owner sees who has not reported yet, with a week-on-week comparison, and gets a reminder at 21:00.

The owner also gets a monthly Excel file with formulas. The Mini App checks Telegram's signed initData (HMAC) before it shows anything. 10 tests cover parsing, reports and the check. Demo mode gives each user a test chain of 4 shops.

SECURITY IN THIS BOT

What keeps this bot safe

Checked in the code on 12 November 2026. Each line says what the guard is and what goes wrong without it.

Mini App sign-in is checked with HMAC

The bot verifies Telegram's signed data as the Telegram docs describe, and checks auth_date so an old signature cannot be replayed. Without this, anyone could open the owner dashboard with a made-up id.

Owner-only views check the owner ids

Totals for every shop are for the owner. A seller who finds the link still gets nothing.

Parameterised SQL

Amounts and names go into queries as values, never as query text.

Edits keep history, "було → стало"

A changed number is never silent. The owner can see what a figure was and what it became.

A suspicious amount asks for confirmation

A total far from the shop's usual day, such as an extra zero, gets "Все правильно?" before it is sent. A typo stops at the seller instead of reaching the report.

Runs in Docker as a non-root user, with 10 tests

If the process were ever taken over, it would not have root rights. The tests cover parsing, reports and the sign-in check.
